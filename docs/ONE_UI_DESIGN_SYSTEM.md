# Fahmino — One UI-inspired design direction

## Product promise
فهمینو باید نقطه شروع و پایان کارهای درسی پایه هشتم باشد: پیدا کردن درس، یادگیری، دیدن PDF، تمرین، آزمون، بررسی اشتباه‌ها و مرور.

## Visual principles
- Calm, uncluttered screens with one clear primary action.
- Large page headings and generous top spacing, inspired by Samsung One UI.
- Rounded surfaces (20–26dp), subtle outlines, no heavy shadows.
- Large touch targets and bottom navigation reachable by one hand.
- Persian RTL and Vazirmatn throughout.
- Consistent light/dark themes; never hard-code surface colors in feature screens.
- Motion should be short, purposeful and respect reduced-motion settings.

## Navigation model
Home, Subjects, Search, Saved, Settings remain persistent. Study helper is reachable from Home and contextual lesson screens.

## Study workflow
1. Search across subjects, chapters, notes, solutions, questions and PDF titles.
2. Open a subject, choose a chapter, then choose lesson/notes/solutions/questions.
3. Read PDF materials in-app where supported; do not substitute long text for requested PDFs.
4. Practice questions, reveal explanations after answering, and save weak areas.
5. Timed exams distribute questions across chapters and keep local result history.

## Content integrity
- Grade 8 only unless the product scope is explicitly changed.
- Distinguish authored content from linked/externally sourced material.
- Use original explanations and question wording; do not copy proprietary question banks.
- Verify each chapter and answer against the current official textbook before release.
- Never label a feature complete merely because its screen exists; verify its full user flow.
