# Fahmino content quality benchmark

This file defines the editorial process for every grade-eight subject and chapter. It is a review checklist, not a source-text repository.

## Required side-by-side review

For each chapter, reviewers maintain a comparison sheet with three columns:

| Reference side | What to inspect | Fahmino side |
|---|---|---|
| Hamyar | Coverage map, explanation sequence, solved examples, question formats, PDF organization | Check the matching chapter's summary, points, example, steps, questions, answer key, and PDF |
| Paadars | Topic coverage, clarity, practice progression, question variety, worked solutions | Check missing subtopics, level progression, feedback and explanations |
| Official curriculum / Samad enrichment | Current textbook objectives; where legitimately available, enrichment objectives and skill domains | Check curriculum alignment and add original enrichment tasks without reproducing protected material |

Record the actual source URL, title, date accessed, chapter/topic, and the observed gap. Do not claim a benchmark was reviewed unless the actual source was opened and inspected.

## Content standard for every chapter

### Notes
- Cover every textbook subtopic and prerequisite needed for the chapter.
- Explain concepts in original Persian, with precise definitions and common misconceptions.
- Include at least two worked examples of different types where the subject allows it.
- Show reasoning step by step, not only the final result.
- Include a compact recap, vocabulary/formulas where relevant, and common mistakes.
- Use accessible examples and do not replace a full lesson with a handful of bullets.

### Question bank
- Build an explicit topic blueprint before writing questions.
- Include recall/understanding, conceptual discrimination, application, multi-step reasoning, interpretation of data/figures, and transfer questions where suitable.
- Progress from foundational to standard to challenging and enrichment levels.
- Avoid duplicate stems, repeated arithmetic with cosmetic changes, irrelevant distractors, ambiguous wording, and answer patterns that reveal the key.
- Give every question a verified answer and a clear explanation. Independently solve it before publication.
- Use original questions. Difficulty must come from reasoning, not obscure wording or trick ambiguity.

### Enrichment / Samad-style challenge
- Add original multi-step, non-routine reasoning and problem-solving tasks.
- Use public curriculum objectives and general skill domains as inspiration; do not copy a Samad worksheet, book, test, diagram, or solution.
- Do not label questions as official Samad questions or imply endorsement.
- Use only content whose license/permission allows reproduction, or link to the official source instead of embedding it.

## Copyright and source policy

1. The official textbook portal is the curriculum anchor: https://chap.sch.ir/
2. Hamyar and Paadars are comparative references for coverage, presentation, and pedagogical quality only. Do not copy their prose, diagrams, question wording, PDF files, answer explanations, or distinctive arrangement.
3. For Samad/SAMPAD, use official public curriculum notices and materials only as allowed by their terms. If no reuse permission is clear, extract only high-level learning objectives and create new questions independently.
4. Attribute linked external PDFs and preserve their original URLs. Do not package third-party PDFs inside Fahmino unless explicit redistribution permission is documented.
5. Maintain a source ledger with URL, publisher, title, date accessed, permitted use, and the exact original Fahmino changes inspired by the source.

## Release gate

A chapter is not complete until:
- all curriculum topics are mapped;
- notes meet the coverage and worked-example checks;
- question types and difficulty levels are balanced;
- every answer and explanation is independently checked;
- generated note/question PDFs open correctly;
- the lesson UI links to the correct chapter PDFs;
- widget, content, asset and build tests pass.
